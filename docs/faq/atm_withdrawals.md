# ATM cash withdrawals

## How much does it cost to withdraw cash?
Cash withdrawals are free up to £200 per month. After that we charge a 2% fee on each
withdrawal.

## What is my daily cash withdrawal limit?
You can withdraw up to £500 per day from ATMs.

## Which ATMs can I use?
You can use any ATM that shows the Visa logo, in any country. Some ATM owners add their
own fee, which the ATM shows on screen before you confirm.

## The ATM kept my card. What should I do?
If an ATM swallowed your card, freeze the card in the app straight away and order a
replacement under Cards > Replace card. Do not wait for the ATM owner to return it.
