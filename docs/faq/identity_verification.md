# Identity verification

## Why do I need to verify my identity?
Banks are required by law to confirm who their customers are, to prevent fraud and money
laundering. You cannot use all account features until your identity is verified.

## Which documents can I use to verify my identity?
You can use a passport, a national identity card or a driving licence. You also take a
selfie in the app so we can match your face to the photo on the document.

## How long does identity verification take?
Most verifications are completed within 10 minutes. Some need a manual review, which
takes up to 1 working day.

## My identity verification failed. What can I do?
Make sure the document is valid, not expired, and the photo is clear without glare.
Take the selfie in good light. You can try again in the app up to 3 times; after that,
an agent will review your documents manually.
