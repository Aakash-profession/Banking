# Lost, stolen or compromised card

## I lost my card or it was stolen. What should I do?
Freeze your card immediately in the app under Cards > Freeze card. Freezing is instant and
you can unfreeze the card if you find it. If the card is really lost or stolen, report it
under Cards > Report lost or stolen. We cancel it and send a new card free of charge.

## I think someone else used my card. What should I do?
Freeze the card in the app straight away, then report the payments you do not recognise
under Activity > Report a payment. Report unrecognised payments within 60 days.

## Will I get my money back for payments I did not make?
If payments were made without your permission and you reported them within 60 days,
we refund them after our investigation, which usually takes up to 10 working days.
