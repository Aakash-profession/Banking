# Card delivery

## How long does it take to receive my new card?
Standard delivery of a new Demo Bank card takes 5 to 7 working days after you order it.
Express delivery takes 2 working days and costs £5.

## Can I track my card delivery?
Yes. Open the app and go to Cards > Track delivery. You will see when the card was posted
and the expected delivery date. Express deliveries also include a courier tracking number.

## My card has not arrived. What should I do?
If your card has not arrived after 10 working days, report it in the app under
Cards > Card not received. We cancel the missing card so nobody else can use it,
and we send you a replacement card free of charge.
