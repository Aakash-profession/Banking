# Exchange rates and currency fees

## What exchange rate do you use?
On weekdays we use the interbank exchange rate, which is the rate banks use with each other.
On weekends the markets are closed, so we add a 1% markup to protect against rate changes.

## Is there a fee for exchanging currency?
You can exchange up to £1,000 per month without a fee. Above that we charge a 0.5% fee
on the amount exchanged.

## Do you charge a fee for card payments abroad?
No. Card payments in a foreign currency have no extra card payment fee. The normal exchange
rate rules above apply.

## The exchange rate on my payment looks wrong. Why?
Card payments use the exchange rate at the moment the shop or ATM sends the payment to us,
which can be a day after you paid. If you paid at the weekend, the weekend markup applies.
