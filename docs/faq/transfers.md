# Bank transfers

## How long does a bank transfer take?
Transfers to other UK bank accounts usually arrive within 2 hours. International
transfers take 1 to 3 working days, depending on the country and currency.

## Is there a fee for sending money?
Transfers to UK accounts are free. International transfers cost 0.5% of the amount,
with a minimum fee of £1.

## Why is my transfer still pending?
Transfers can stay pending while we run security checks, or when they are sent outside
working hours. International transfers sent on a Friday may arrive on Monday or Tuesday.

## Can I cancel a transfer?
You can cancel a transfer only while it is still pending: open it in the app and tap
Cancel transfer. Completed transfers cannot be cancelled; contact the recipient instead.
