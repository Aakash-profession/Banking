# Topping up your account

## How can I top up my account?
You can top up with a debit card, by bank transfer to your Demo Bank account details,
or with Apple Pay or Google Pay. Cash and cheque top-ups are not supported.

## What are the top-up limits?
The minimum top-up is £10. You can top up at most £5,000 per day and £20,000 per month.

## Are there fees for topping up?
Top-ups by bank transfer are free. Top-ups by debit card are free up to £3,000 per month;
above that we charge a 0.5% fee.

## Why is my top-up pending or why did it fail?
Card top-ups usually arrive within a few minutes but can stay pending for up to 1 hour.
Bank transfer top-ups take up to 1 working day. If a top-up fails, check that the card is
in your own name and has not expired. Money from a failed top-up is returned to your card
within 5 working days.
