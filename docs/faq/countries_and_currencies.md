# Supported countries, currencies and cards

## Which countries do you support?
You can open a Demo Bank account if you live in the UK or in a country of the European
Economic Area (EEA). You can use your card in every country where Visa is accepted.

## Which currencies can I hold?
You can hold and exchange 30 currencies in the app, including GBP, EUR, USD, INR and JPY.

## Is my card a Visa or a Mastercard?
All Demo Bank cards are Visa debit cards.

## Which cards can I use to top up?
You can top up with Visa or Mastercard debit cards in your own name. Credit cards and
prepaid cards are not accepted for top-ups.
