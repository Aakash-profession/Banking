# Account basics

## How old do I need to be to open an account?
You must be at least 18 years old to open a Demo Bank account.

## Can I use Apple Pay or Google Pay?
Yes. Add your Demo Bank card to Apple Pay or Google Pay from Cards > Add to wallet in the
app. Payments with your phone work wherever contactless payments are accepted.

## How do I change my personal details?
You can change your address, email and phone number in the app under Profile > Personal
details. A change of legal name needs a new identity document.

## How do I close my account?
Move all your money out first, so the balance is zero. Then go to Profile > Close account
in the app. Closing is free and takes effect immediately.
