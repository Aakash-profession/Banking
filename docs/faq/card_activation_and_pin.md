# Card activation and PIN

## How do I activate my card?
Open the app, go to Cards > Activate card and enter the 6-digit code printed on the letter
that came with your card. The card works immediately after activation.

## Where do I find my PIN?
You choose your own 4-digit PIN during activation in the app. You can view it any time
under Cards > Show PIN after confirming your identity with your fingerprint or passcode.

## How can I change my PIN?
You can change your PIN at any ATM that offers a "change PIN" option. The new PIN works
straight away for chip and PIN payments.

## My PIN is blocked. What can I do?
Your PIN is blocked after 3 wrong attempts in a row. You can unblock it in the app under
Cards > Unblock PIN. If the app does not allow it, an agent can unblock it for you.
